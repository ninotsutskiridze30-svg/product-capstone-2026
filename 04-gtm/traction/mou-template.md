## Purpose

This Letter of Intent records the Tutor's non-binding commitment to participate as an early tutor on the Sakheli platform during the June 2026 Georgian national exam preparation season, and the Platform's commitments to the Tutor for that period.

## Tutor commitments

The Tutor commits to:

1. **List a tutor profile** on https://tutoring-lyart.vercel.app within 7 days of signing, including subject(s) taught, hourly rate, and at least 3 hours/week of bookable availability.
2. **Conduct at least 5 lessons** through the Platform during the period 1 June 2026 – 15 July 2026.
3. **Provide informal feedback** on the platform experience after the first 3 lessons, by message or short call.

## Platform commitments

The Platform commits to:

1. **Take a 15% commission** on lessons booked through the Platform during the LOI period — no listing fees, no subscription, no other charges.
2. **Cover the cost** of any lesson where the student fails to attend without 24 hours' notice (the Tutor still earns their fee for the slot).
3. **Provide direct support** from the founder by Telegram or WhatsApp within 24 hours for any platform-related issue.
4. **Not contact the Tutor's students** outside of the lessons they are booked into — the tutor-student relationship belongs to the Tutor.

## Term and termination

This LOI runs from the date of signing until 15 July 2026. Either party may withdraw at any time with written notice (a message is sufficient). This LOI is **non-binding** and does not create a contractual or employment relationship between the parties.
