# Tutor Discovery Experiment

## Hypothesis

Students prefer structured tutor discovery platforms over informal social media searching.

## Experiment

The team tested the MVP with university students and observed how users searched for tutors.

## Results

Users reported:
- faster tutor discovery
- easier navigation
- higher trust compared to Facebook groups

Several users specifically mentioned that organized tutor profiles improved confidence.

## Decision

Persevere with the tutoring marketplace direction.
