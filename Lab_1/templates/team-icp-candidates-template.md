## Candidate ICP 1

**Linked to problem:** KIU students miss lectures due to timetable changes in LMS without proper notifications 
**Submitted by (original problem author):** Nino Tsutskiridze

---

### 1.1 Who Is This Person?

**Segment label:** KIU undergraduate students using LMS

**Demographics (as relevant to the problem):**  
Age 18–24. Students at Kutaisi International University or similar universities in Georgia. Enrolled in multiple courses that rely on LMS for schedules and announcements.

**Psychographics (as relevant to the problem):**  
Students want to stay organized and not miss important classes or quizzes. They rely on digital systems but expect them to be reliable. They feel frustrated when they miss something important due to lack of communication.

---

### 1.2 Their Context

**Where do they experience this problem?**  
On the LMS platform or when checking schedules at home or before going to university.

**When does this problem occur?**  
When timetable changes are made (e.g., quiz time changes or lecture rescheduling) without clear notification.

**How often?**  
Occasionally during the semester, especially during busy periods like midterms.

---

### 1.3 Their Current Workaround

**What do they do today to cope with this problem?**  
They ask classmates, check group chats (Messenger/WhatsApp), or manually check LMS frequently.

**Why is the current workaround inadequate?**  
It depends on other people and is unreliable. Students may still miss important updates, which can affect attendance or grades.

---

### 1.4 Why This Segment

**Why is this a promising target segment?**  
Students are easy to reach and the problem is directly experienced by many people in the same environment. Missing lectures or quizzes has clear academic consequences, so they are motivated to solve it

**What would need to be true for this to be a strong ICP?**  
We need to confirm how often this happens across different students and whether most students rely on LMS notifications or alternative communication methods.

---

### 1.5 Confidence Assessment

**Confidence in this ICP:** Medium

**What is this confidence based on?**  
Personal experience and conversations with classmates who faced similar issues.

**What would increase your confidence?**  
Interviews with multiple students to confirm how often timetable changes are missed and how they currently manage updates.

## Candidate ICP 2

**Linked to problem:** Students in Tbilisi struggle to find affordable rental housing at the start of the semester   
**Submitted by (original problem author):** Lizi Margvelashvili

---

### 2.1 Who Is This Person?

**Segment label:** University students searching for rental housing

**Demographics (relevant to the problem):**  
Age 18–25. Students studying in Tbilisi or Kutaisi. Limited income or dependent on family support. Looking for shared or budget apartments.

**Psychographics (relevant to the problem):**  
Students want affordable and comfortable housing close to university. They feel stressed and pressured when searching for apartments, especially before the semester starts.

---

### 2.2 Their Context

**Where do they experience this problem?**  
On Facebook Marketplace, rental websites, and through personal connections when searching for apartments.

**When does this problem occur?**  
At the beginning of the semester or when moving to a new city.

**How often?**  
Once or twice per year, but very intense during that period.


---

### 2.3 Their Current Workaround

**What do they do today to cope?**  
They search across multiple platforms, contact many landlords, share apartments with roommates, or settle for lower-quality housing.

**Why is the current workaround inadequate?**  
It is time-consuming, stressful, and often leads to poor living conditions or high costs.

---

### 2.4 Why This Segment

**Why is this a promising target?**  
This is a large group of students facing a real financial and social problem. The impact is significant because housing affects daily life and education.

**What would need to be true for this to be a strong ICP?**  
We need to understand whether students would use a digital solution and what features they actually need (price comparison, verified listings, etc.).

---

### 2.5 Confidence Assessment

**Confidence in this ICP:** Medium 

**What is this confidence based on?**  
Observations from friends and classmates experiencing housing difficulties.

**What would increase your confidence?**  
Interviews with students to understand their search process, challenges, and willingness to use new tools.
