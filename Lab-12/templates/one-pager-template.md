## HEADER SECTION

**Product name:** TutorLink

**Tagline:** Find a trusted tutor in Georgia — today, not next week.

**Live URL:** tutoring-lyart.vercel.app

---

## PROBLEM SECTION

University students in Georgia spend one to six weeks finding a private tutor through WhatsApp group chats — with no visibility into availability, rates, or quality until after direct contact. One in four students abandons the search entirely; in our research, one student failed her first exam as a direct result.

---

## SOLUTION SECTION

TutorLink lets students search for tutors by subject and language, see real rates and available slots, read peer reviews, and confirm a booking in a single session — no group chat posts required.
