## ICP Screener Reminder

**Primary ICP:** University students at KIU who have searched for a private tutor in the past semester and found the process difficult or time-consuming.

**Secondary ICP:** Parents in Tbilisi paying for private tutoring for a secondary school child with no reliable way to assess whether sessions are producing progress.

**Tutor-side (emerging from data):** Active private tutors who receive inbound messages from students and must decline the majority — flagged after Interview 5 as a supply-side ICP worth pursuing.

## Outreach Message Templates

### Template A — Primary ICP (Student who searched for a tutor)

"Hey [name], I am working on a university research project about how students find tutors. I think you have been through that search before — would you have 20 minutes this week to tell me about it? It is a real conversation, not a survey, and there is nothing to sign up for. Happy to meet on campus or do a quick call."

### Template B — Tutor-side (Active private tutor)

"Hi [name], I am doing research for a university project about how private tutoring works in Georgia — specifically how students find tutors and how tutors manage student requests. I know you tutor privately and I would love to hear about your experience from that side. Would you have 20 minutes this week? Completely informal, no recording unless you are comfortable with it."

### Template C — Secondary ICP (Parent)

"Hello, my name is [name] and I am a student at KIU working on a research project about private tutoring. I am hoping to speak with parents who have hired a private tutor for their child. The conversation would take about 20 minutes and there is nothing to sign up for — I just want to understand the experience from a parent's perspective. I can speak in Georgian or English."

## Progress Notes

**What is working:**
Direct WhatsApp outreach to students who are personally known to team members is converting quickly — all 5 completed interviews were scheduled within 24 hours of first contact. Personal context ("I saw you posted looking for a tutor") is the most effective opening.

**What is harder:**
Parent segment requires warmer introductions and longer lead times. Cold outreach to parents has not been attempted yet — will build the parent pipeline through Lizi's neighbour referrals and Mari's former student family contact.

**Supply side (tutors):**
Interview 5 (MT) confirmed the supply-side problem is real and worth pursuing. We now have one completed tutor-side interview and two more planned (contacts #6 and #12). We need at least 2–3 more tutor interviews before Lab 4 to have enough supply-side data for the affinity map.

**International student segment:**
Interview 4 (SN) produced the most severe case in the dataset. We should identify at least one more international student interviewee to confirm whether the language barrier finding is consistent or specific to SN.
