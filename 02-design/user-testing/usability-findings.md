# Usability Findings

## Summary

The team conducted usability testing sessions with students to evaluate the TutorLink MVP.

## Key Findings

### 1. Structured tutor profiles increased trust

Users reported feeling more confident when tutor profiles included detailed descriptions and subjects.

### 2. Navigation was intuitive

Most users completed the tutor discovery flow without assistance.

### 3. Multilanguage support improved accessibility

Participants appreciated the ability to switch languages easily.

### 4. Users wanted clearer pricing visibility

Several users suggested displaying pricing information earlier in the browsing process.

### 5. Students preferred the platform over social media searching

Participants stated that organized tutor listings felt faster and more reliable than Facebook groups or Telegram chats.

## Product Decisions

Based on the testing sessions, the team decided to continue improving:

* tutor profile detail
* trust-focused design
* search and filtering experience
