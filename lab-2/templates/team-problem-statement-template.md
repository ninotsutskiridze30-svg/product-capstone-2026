## The Problem Statement

> Second- and third-year university students in Tbilisi who are searching for rental housing before the semester experience high stress and uncertainty when trying to find affordable and reliable apartment options, causing them to spend excessive time searching, accept poor living conditions, or pay more than they can afford. Currently they cope by browsing multiple Facebook groups, rental websites, and messaging landlords directly, which is inadequate because listings are often outdated, misleading, and require significant time and effort to verify.

---

## Breakdown

**Who has the problem (specific user segment):**  
Second- and third-year university students in Tbilisi who need to rent housing before the semester starts.

---

**What exactly goes wrong (the pain):**  
Students feel stressed, overwhelmed, and uncertain because they cannot find trustworthy and affordable housing options.

---

**When and where it happens (context):**  
Before the semester begins, when students search for housing online using Facebook groups, rental websites, or personal networks.

---

**What it costs them (consequence):**  
Students waste time searching, experience stress, and often make poor decisions such as choosing more expensive or lower-quality housing.

---

**What they do about it today (current workaround):**  
They browse multiple Facebook groups, search rental websites, message landlords individually, and ask friends for recommendations.

---

**Why the workaround is inadequate:**  
These methods are inefficient, unreliable, and time-consuming because listings are often outdated, misleading, or unavailable.
