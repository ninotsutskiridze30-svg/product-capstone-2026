## Primary ICP: Student housing problem

### 1. Segment

Second- and third-year university students in Tbilisi (e.g., KIU students) who are not from the city and need to rent apartments before the semester starts. They typically have limited budgets and often share apartments with roommates.

---

### 2. Context

The problem occurs before the start of each semester, when students are searching for housing online or through personal networks. It typically happens at home, on their phone or laptop, while browsing Facebook groups, rental websites, or messaging people.

---

### 3. Primary Pain

Students feel stressed and overwhelmed because they cannot find reliable, affordable housing options. Listings are often outdated, misleading, or too expensive, which creates uncertainty and frustration.

---

### 4. Current Workaround

Students search across multiple Facebook groups, rental websites, and ask friends or classmates for recommendations. They also message many landlords manually and sometimes accept poor housing conditions due to lack of better options.

---

### 5. Job to Be Done

When I need to find housing before the semester starts, I want to quickly find affordable and trustworthy rental options, so I can secure a comfortable place to live without stress or financial pressure.

---

### 6. Success Signal

If this problem were solved, students would spend less time searching, avoid unreliable listings, and feel confident in their housing choice. They would experience less stress and make better financial decisions.

---

### 7. Where to Find Them

- KIU campus (cafeteria, study areas)  
- Facebook housing groups in Tbilisi  
- Student WhatsApp and Messenger groups  
- Friends and classmates currently renting  

---

### 8. Why You, Why Now

We are university students ourselves and have direct access to this group. Many of our classmates and friends are currently renting or searching for housing, making it easy to reach them and conduct interviews.

## Secondary ICP: LMS timetable issue

### 1. Segment

Undergraduate students at KIU who rely on LMS for schedules and announcements.

---

### 2. Context

The problem occurs during the semester when schedule changes are made in the LMS, often before lectures or quizzes.

---

### 3. Primary Pain

Students miss important updates and feel confused or frustrated when they arrive at the wrong time or miss lectures.

---

### 4. Current Workaround

Students frequently check LMS manually or rely on classmates and group chats for updates.

---

### 5. Job to Be Done

When my class schedule changes, I want to be clearly notified, so I can attend lectures on time and avoid missing important academic activities.

---

### 6. Success Signal

Students would feel confident that they are always aware of schedule changes and would no longer need to rely on others or constantly check the system.

---

### 7. Where to Find Them

- KIU campus  
- Class group chats  
- LMS users (students)  

---

### 8. Why You, Why Now

We are part of this group and experience this issue directly, giving us easy access to interview participants.
