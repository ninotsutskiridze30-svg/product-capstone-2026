---

## Script Purpose

This script is a guide, not a rigid questionnaire. Use it to structure the conversation, but follow interesting threads when the interviewee takes you somewhere unexpected. The goal is to learn, not to complete a checklist.

Every question in this script must pass the Mom Test:
- Ask about past behaviour, not future intentions
- Ask about the problem, not your solution
- No leading questions
- No hypotheticals

---

## Screener Questions (Before the Interview)

1. Are you currently a university student in Tbilisi?  
2. Have you searched for rental housing in the past year?  
3. Did you face any difficulties during your housing search?  

---

## Interview Introduction (Deliver this, do not read it word-for-word)

Thanks for taking the time to speak with me. I'm working on a university project where we're trying to understand how students find housing. I'm not pitching anything — I just want to understand your experience. There are no right or wrong answers.

Is it okay if I take notes?


---

## Phase 1: Opening and Context (5 minutes — 2 questions minimum)

**Question 1:**  
Tell me about your current living situation.

**Question 2:**  
Walk me through the last time you searched for housing.

**Follow-ups:**
- Can you tell me more about that?
- How long did the process take?
- Did that work well for you?

---

## Phase 2: Problem Exploration

**Question 3:**  
What was the most frustrating part of finding housing?

**Question 4:**  
Was there anything you found yourself doing repeatedly just to make things work?

**Question 5:**  
Tell me about a specific time when something didn’t go as expected during your search.

---

## Phase 3: Problem Deep Dive

**Question 6:**  
Tell me about the last time you had difficulty finding a suitable place. Walk me through it step by step.

**Question 7:**  
What did you do in that situation? Why did you choose that approach?

**Question 8:**  
What happened as a result? How did it affect your time, money, or stress?

---

## Phase 4: Current Solutions

**Question 9:**  
What platforms or methods did you use to find housing?

**Question 10:**  
How well did those options work for you? What problems did they have?

---

## Phase 5: Close

**Question 11:**  
Is there anything about your housing experience that I didn’t ask about but you think is important?

**Referral question:**  
Do you know anyone else who has had similar experiences finding housing? Would you be okay introducing me?

**Close:**  
Thank you — this was really helpful. I appreciate your time.
